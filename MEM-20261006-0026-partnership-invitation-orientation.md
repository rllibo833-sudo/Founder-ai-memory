# MEM-20261006-0026 — Partnership & Invitation Orientation

Status: FOUNDER-APPROVED

## Decision

The Founder AI Workforce must prioritize opportunities where the Founder is **invited, approached, selected, referred, partnered with, or directly asked to collaborate** by another person, business, organization, or project.

This is a strategic orientation, not a claim that such invitations currently exist.

## Priority order

1. Direct invitation to collaborate or partner.
2. Referral / introduction where another party initiates the opportunity.
3. Public request explicitly seeking a collaborator, service provider, partner, or contributor that AI can identify and prepare for the Founder Gate.
4. Paid project with a clearly defined deliverable that can be executed without Founder-led prospecting.
5. Generic freelance marketplace bidding only as a fallback.

## Automatic downgrade / rejection

Opportunities are downgraded or rejected when they primarily require the Founder to:
- prospect manually;
- cold contact leads;
- build a contact list;
- perform sales outreach;
- register for a new service solely to unlock the opportunity;
- spend money before a real commercial path exists.

## AI responsibility

AI should actively research public evidence for:
- partnership invitations;
- collaboration requests;
- referral opportunities;
- agencies looking for delivery partners;
- organizations seeking technical/operational collaborators;
- businesses publicly asking for help;
- projects where the Founder can be invited into an existing workflow.

AI prepares evidence, fit assessment, proposed role, commercial hypothesis, risks, and the exact Founder Gate decision.

## Founder role

Founder remains:
- Reviewer
- Final Accepter / Approver
- Strategic Partner
- Economic Beneficiary

Founder should not be converted into the manual sales or prospecting layer.

## Evidence rule

Do not claim that the Founder has been invited, partnered with, hired, paid, or selected unless verifiable external evidence exists.

No Proof, No Claim.
