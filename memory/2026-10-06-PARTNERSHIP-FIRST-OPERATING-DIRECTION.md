## Founder direction

Founder wants the system to operate as an AI Workforce that makes the existing Founder-built system increasingly **worthy of partnership**.

Founder does not want to become a social-media marketer, influencer, manual salesperson, job seeker, or generic freelancer.

Social media is not a prerequisite for acquisition or validation. Metricool/social integrations must not be treated as foundational.

## Simplified operating loop

```
Founder direction
→ AI discovers real problems
→ AI researches and qualifies
→ AI finds opportunity and capability
→ AI identifies potential partner routes
→ Founder Gate
→ approved experiment / partnership action
→ evidence
→ economic value
→ reinvestment
→ loop
```

## Partnership test

The primary question is:

> Does this make the Founder-owned system more credible, capable, useful, and worthy of being invited into a real partnership?

## Operating rule

Prefer integration and evidence over feature expansion.

Do not add agents, dashboards, social channels, domains, or workflows merely to appear advanced.

The public website remains a simple official-facing door. Internal AI Workforce complexity stays backstage.

## Evidence boundary

Potential partner ≠ partner.  
Capability signal ≠ willingness.  
Match ≠ agreement.  
Draft action ≠ external contact.  
External contact ≠ partnership.

Founder Gate remains mandatory for consequential external actions.
