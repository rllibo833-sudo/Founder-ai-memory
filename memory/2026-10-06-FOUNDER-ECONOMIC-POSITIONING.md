## Canonical decision

Founder remains the **solo founder, system owner, strategic project partner, final approver, and economic beneficiary**.

Founder is not positioned as a job seeker, employee applicant, generic freelancer, manual salesperson, or person personally seeking funding.

The system is designed so that Founder owns the vision, product/system concept, intellectual direction, and execution architecture, while qualified institutions, companies, researchers, technical teams, and other capability holders may be invited into specific partnerships.

## Operating model

```
Founder / System Owner
→ AI Workforce discovery and research
→ qualification
→ capability / partner matching
→ project or partnership structure
→ Founder Gate
→ execution
→ evidence
→ economic outcome
→ reinvestment
```

Funding is an instrument attached to a qualified project, not the Founder's identity.

## Evidence boundary

Candidate ≠ partner.  
Public capability ≠ willingness.  
Invitation draft ≠ contact.  
Contact ≠ interest.  
Interest ≠ agreement.  
Agreement ≠ funded project.  
Funding approval ≠ cash received.  
Opportunity ≠ customer.  
Proposal ≠ revenue.  
Delivery ≠ paid without payment evidence.

## Founder Gate

No external contact, application, binding proposal, spending, sensitive-data sharing, legal/IP commitment, partner appointment, or external representation occurs without Founder approval.

## Strategic test

Every agent, workflow, feature, or opportunity should answer:

> Does this increase the AI Workforce's ability to discover, qualify, orchestrate, execute, verify, or compound economic opportunities for the Founder-owned ecosystem?
