# MEM-20261006-0020 — Founder Reviewer/Accepter Boundary and AI Opportunity Loop

Status: FOUNDER-APPROVED
Date: 2026-10-06
Scope: Founder AI operating model

## Decision

The Founder explicitly defines their operating boundary as **Reviewer and Accepter**, not as a FOMO/vibe coder and not as the manual prospect-searching layer.

The AI Workforce is expected to operate the economic discovery loop:

**DISCOVER → RESEARCH → QUALIFY → PREPARE → EXECUTE WHERE PERMITTED → VERIFY → LEARN → REPEAT**

## Founder role

The Founder:
- reviews evidence-backed opportunities;
- accepts, rejects, or requests revision;
- approves consequential external representation;
- approves commercial commitments and paid offers;
- approves sensitive-data movement and other irreversible actions;
- accepts verified delivery/revenue evidence and margin conclusions.

The Founder should not be routinely assigned manual lead generation, prospect searching, repetitive market research, or FOMO/vibe-coding tasks when the AI can perform the work.

## AI role

AI should:
- search for real opportunities using permitted external sources;
- research the context and underlying problem;
- qualify opportunities against the selected wedge;
- prepare briefs, questions, offers, scope, pricing hypotheses, and delivery plans;
- execute delegated digital/operational work where permitted;
- collect and validate evidence;
- calculate revenue, direct costs, and margin when payment/delivery evidence exists;
- learn from outcomes and continue the loop.

AI must not fabricate prospects, users, customers, agreements, payments, revenue, or margin, and must not self-accept consequential actions on behalf of the Founder.

## Economic principle

The project is not successful because the repository contains more features or because activity appears in a dashboard.

The target loop is:

**real opportunity → evidence → Founder acceptance → real external action → useful delivery → payment → verified margin → repeatable loop**

A blank tracker remains valid when no real evidence exists.
