## Magnet requirement

All three repositories must function as a **magnet**, but each attracts through a different layer:

**MEMORY attracts understanding and trust.**

**CORE attracts technical, research and operational interest through evidence and working capability.**

**PUBLIC attracts people, needs, opportunities and contributors through a clear human-facing entry point.**

A repository is not a magnet merely because it has commits, README text, issues, or activity.

A magnet must create this progression:

**DISCOVER → UNDERSTAND → SEE EVIDENCE → RECOGNIZE USEFULNESS → BRING A NEED / OFFER CONTRIBUTION → QUALIFY → FOUNDER GATE → REAL OUTPUT → PROOF → REPUTATION → MORE INBOUND**

## Phase model

### Phase 1 — Discover
AI searches public evidence, opportunities, collaboration pathways, technical needs and relevant signals.

Automation: **YES**

### Phase 2 — Research
AI researches questions and collects sources.

Automation: **YES**

### Phase 3 — Evidence
AI structures source provenance, evidence and limitations.

Automation: **YES**

### Phase 4 — Analysis
AI analyzes evidence and prepares decision material.

Automation: **YES**

### Phase 5 — Qualification
AI triages requests/opportunities against capability, fit, risk, deliverable clarity and Founder constraints.

Automation: **YES**

### Phase 6 — Match
AI matches qualified needs with available capabilities, workflows or contributors.

Automation: **YES**

### Phase 7 — Founder Gate
Founder decides whether to accept/reject, scope, price, deadline and external commitment.

Automation: **NO — HUMAN CONTROL**

### Phase 8 — Deliver
AI can research, draft, implement, test and prepare deliverables. Consequential external actions remain subject to Founder control.

Automation: **PARTIAL**

### Phase 9 — Acceptance
A real external recipient must accept the result.

Automation: **NO — REAL-WORLD EVENT**

### Phase 10 — Payment
A real payment must occur and be verifiable.

Automation: **PARTIAL**

### Phase 11 — Margin
Revenue, costs and verified/explainable margin can be calculated after a real transaction.

Automation: **PARTIAL / AUTOMATABLE AFTER REAL INPUT**

### Phase 12 — Proof
Real outcomes, acceptance, payment and evidence are recorded.

Automation: **PARTIAL**

### Phase 13 — Real-world pilot
A real person, organization or field workflow must use the system.

Automation: **NO — HUMAN/WORLD DEPENDENT**

### Phase 14 — Contributors / collaboration
The system can attract, qualify and prepare collaboration pathways; people decide whether to participate.

Automation: **PARTIAL**

### Phase 15 — Scale
Scaling follows verified proof, economics and real demand.

Automation: **NO — STRATEGIC GATE**
