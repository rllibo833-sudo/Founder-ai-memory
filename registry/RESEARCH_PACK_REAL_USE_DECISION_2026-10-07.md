# RESEARCH PACK — REAL-USE DECISION

Status: **FOUNDER-APPROVED / ACTIVE**

The first external capability remains **AI-assisted Operations / Research Pack**.

This is a real-use service boundary, not a demo mode.

For legal/regulatory research, the system may produce:

- relevant regulations from official sources;
- source/provenance register;
- evidence extracted from sources;
- questions to take to a qualified legal professional;
- documentation/verification checklist;
- unresolved items marked UNVERIFIED / NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW;
- explicit limitations.

It must not provide legal opinions, legal determinations, compliance guarantees, or replace qualified counsel.
