# MEM-20261006-0027 — Public Discoverability & Capability Partnership

**Status:** FOUNDER-APPROVED  
**Date:** 2026-10-06

## Decision

The Kawasan Masjid 1.000 Ha system should be built so capable individuals and organizations can discover the project and choose to approach the Founder to strengthen it through a concrete partnership, collaboration, capability contribution, or pilot.

This is an inbound discoverability strategy, not a manual prospecting strategy.

## Founder Boundary

Founder remains:
- solo founder;
- system owner;
- strategic project partner;
- reviewer;
- final accepter/approver;
- economic beneficiary.

Founder does not become the manual discovery, cold outreach, lead-generation, or account-registration layer.

## AI Responsibility

AI Workforce should:
1. continuously research public evidence for relevant opportunities and capability partners;
2. improve the public project surface so its real capabilities, gaps, evidence, and collaboration model are understandable;
3. prepare partnership/collaboration briefs for Founder review;
4. preserve the evidence boundary between candidate, invitation, interest, agreement, and actual economic outcome;
5. operate using supported free-tier infrastructure where practical.

## Preferred Inbound Partner Categories

- AI/agent engineering
- product and UX
- research/evaluation
- data/automation
- security/privacy
- domain expertise
- institutional/legal-entity capability
- pilot environments
- ecosystem/distribution capability
- strategic co-development

## Continuous Loop

DISCOVER → RESEARCH → QUALIFY → MATCH → PREPARE → FOUNDER GATE → APPROVED ACTION → OUTCOME → EVIDENCE → ECONOMIC RESULT → LEARNING → DISCOVER AGAIN

The loop may operate through scheduled/event-driven jobs. It must not bypass provider quotas, abuse free services, spam, impersonate, or make consequential commitments without Founder approval.

## Public Evidence Boundary

- Public capability ≠ willingness to partner
- Candidate ≠ partner
- Invitation draft ≠ contact
- Contact ≠ interest
- Interest ≠ agreement
- Agreement ≠ funded project
- Funding approval ≠ cash received
- Payment ≠ verified margin

## Current Economic Truth

No partnership, customer, payment, revenue, or verified margin may be claimed without external evidence.

**No Proof, No Claim.**
